Subject: URGENT - carton labelled 5 mg contains 10 mg blisters, Thyronex, batch THX-26011

To whom it may concern,

During a routine stock check this morning our pharmacy technician opened a carton of
Thyronex 5 mg Tablets, batch THX-26011, expiry 2028-01-31, and found that the blister strips
inside are printed "Thyronex 10 mg". The outer carton and the patient information leaflet both
say 5 mg. The blisters are otherwise sealed and undamaged.

We checked the rest of the delivery: 4 cartons out of 20 from this batch contain 10 mg blisters
in a 5 mg carton. The other 16 appear correct.

Thyronex is a narrow therapeutic index product and a doubling of the dose would be clinically
significant. No units from this delivery have been dispensed to patients - the discrepancy was
found before the stock was released. All 20 cartons have been quarantined and labelled
"DO NOT DISPENSE".

We consider this a potential product mix-up and request an urgent response, including whether
other batches or sites are affected.
