Subject: Visible particles in Onkovex 100 mg/10 ml injection - batch OVX-26042

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a senior nurse in the oncology day unit at Riverside Oncology Centre. Today, while
preparing an infusion, I inspected a vial of Onkovex 100 mg/10 ml Solution for Injection
(batch OVX-26042, expiry 2027-04-30) against a light box and saw several small floating
particles, grey and fibre-like, in the solution. The vial was within expiry, the flip-off cap
and the stopper were intact, and there was no discoloration of the liquid.

We inspected the remaining vials from the same carton and found visible particles in 2 of the
10 vials. All 10 vials have been withdrawn from use and are stored at 2-8 degrees Celsius as
per the label, in a monitored fridge with no recorded temperature excursion.

No product from these vials was administered to any patient, so there is no adverse event to
report at this time.

Given that this is a sterile parenteral product used in immunocompromised patients, we would
like confirmation of the sterility status of this batch and information on whether other units
have been affected.
